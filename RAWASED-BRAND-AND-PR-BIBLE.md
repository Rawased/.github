## 1. Brand Architecture & Etymology

### 1.1 Rawased (`رواصد`) - The Financial Filing & Valuation Platform
* **Arabic Root:** `ر - ص - د` (*Ra-Sa-Da* - to observe closely, to track from the source, to record with precision) with phonetic resonance to `رواسي` (*Rawaasi* - unshakeable mountain anchors).
* **Meaning in Markets:** Price feeds copy summary tables and miss what sits inside the notes. Rawased reads the primary filing first, reconciles every footnote to the halala, and builds the debt, Zakat, and valuation models from the audited numbers.
* **Role:** The flagship company (`rawased.com`), live financial terminal (`app.rawased.com`), Python SDK (`rawased-python`), and 18-tool MCP server (`rawased-mcp`).
* **Founders:** Waleed Alogayel (`وليد العقيِّل` · `waleed@rawased.com`) and Abdulmohsen Alhassan (`عبدالمحسن الحسن` · `abdulmohsen@rawased.com`).

### 1.2 Byan (`بيان`) - The Personal Wealth & Zakat Purification Sister Brand
* **Arabic Root:** `ب - ي - ن` (*Ba-Ya-Na* - clarity, proof, clear financial statement).
* **Role:** The consumer personal-wealth, dividend income, and per-share Zakat purification application. Connected to Rawased via **Bridge 05**, where Rawased computes corporate Zakat and purification ratios from audited Tadawul footnotes and syncs them directly to Byan.

## 4. The Rawased Voice & Copy System (`VOICE-KERNEL.md` + `theyll-never-know-ai-wrote-it-distill.md`)

### 4.1 The Character & Voice Posture ("We Are / We Are Not")

**Who is speaking:** An analyst who has actually opened a 140-page Tadawul PDF at midnight, found a 26.45 million SAR gap between Note 14 and the broker feed, and rebuilt the debt schedule before an investment committee meeting. We speak to other analysts, CFOs, and students as peers.

| We Are | We Are Not |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Problem-first:** We start with the exact place where a spreadsheet or feed breaks. | **Slogan-first:** We never write three-part AI taglines (*"Primary Filings. Exact Arithmetic. Zero Noise."*). |
| **Readable aloud:** A finance student can read any sentence aloud in one breath, and a parent can understand the point. | **Jargon-stuffed:** We never cram eight acronyms and four numbers into one 40-word breathless clause. |
| **Second-person & active:** We use *"you"* and active verbs (*pull up, open, check, split, deduct*). | **Passive & grandiose:** We never call ourselves a *"Sovereign Financial Observatory"* or *"Complete Underwriting Architecture"*. |
| **Native in both languages:** English and Arabic are written separately for the ear of each language. | **Word-for-word translated:** We never translate English marketing idioms literally into stiff Arabic. |
| **Strict on boundaries:** We show the math, cite the note, and leave the buy/sell call to the analyst. | **Advisory or hyped:** We never output price targets, `BUY`/`SELL` ratings, or retail trading hype. |

### 4.2 Six Standing Copy Laws
1. **The Read-Aloud & Parent Test (`VOICE-KERNEL.md` Rule 4):** Every headline and lead must be readable aloud by a student without running out of breath and understood by a parent on the first read. No corporate grandeur.
2. **One Idea Per Sentence (`quwwa` + Book Ch. 4 Yes List):** Keep standard sentences under 18 words. When you write a longer sentence to explain a cause (`because`, `which means`), follow it immediately with a short 4-to-8-word sentence (**Burstiness: The Cut, The Stretch, The Drop**).
3. **One Concrete Detail Per Paragraph (Book Ch. 3.2):** Anchor each paragraph in **one** specific real-world fact (such as Luberef `2223.SR` Q3 Note 14 showing SAR 238.45M vs. SAR 212.00M on the feed) rather than dumping a laundry list of tickers and acronyms into one line.
4. **Arabic and English Written Separately (`najdi`):** Never mix Arabic and English words in the same prose sentence, and never translate word-for-word. English is direct analyst prose; Arabic is natural Saudi financial prose aligned with Tadawul (`تداول`), CMA (`هيئة السوق المالية`), and ZATCA (`هيئة الزكاة والضريبة والجمارك`).
5. **Zero Em Dashes or En Dashes (`RULES-CARD.md`):** Never use `—` or `–` in any headline, body paragraph, code comment, or UI label. Use periods, colons, or commas.
6. **The Rawased No List (`replyvoice.py --lint`):** Ban all statistical-mean AI vocabulary (`delve`, `tapestry`, `unleash`, `unlock`, `ever-evolving`, `dynamic landscape`, `testament`, `synergy`, `robust`, `paradigm shift`, `impactful`, `seamless`, `empower`), transitional adverb openers (`Additionally,`, `Furthermore,`, `Consequently,`, `Moreover,`), SaaS template clichés (`Start Free. Scale from...`, `without spreadsheet blindspots`), and summary closers (`In conclusion`, `To summarize`).

## 5. The 5 Institutional Financial & AI Bridges (`/bridges` & `/api/v1/bridges/{symbol}`)

1. **Bridge 01 - Primary Filing vs. Market Feed Reconciliation Bridge (`DISAGREES` Engine):**
   Compares every line item in audited Tadawul PDF footnotes and SEC XBRL disclosures against commercial market feeds (`Delta_bps = |Audited - Vendor| / |Audited| * 10,000`), flagging any divergence above `50 bps` and citing the exact audited note and page number.
2. **Bridge 02 - Saudi ZATCA Corporate Zakat (`2.5%` / `2.577%`) to NOPAT, ROIC & EVA Bridge:**
   Replaces flat global corporate tax assumptions with Saudi mixed-ownership Zakat (`2.5%` Hijri / `2.577%` Gregorian on Saudi/GCC ownership) and foreign income tax (`20%` on non-GCC ownership) to compute true Zakat-Adjusted `NOPAT`, `ROIC`, and Economic Value Added (`EVA`).
3. **Bridge 03 - Enterprise Value (`EV`) to Implied Equity Value Bridge (`CAST` + `IFRS 16` + `IAS 19 EOSB`):**
   Bridges `Market Cap -> EV -> Implied Equity Value -> Fair Value Per Share` by deducting Islamic Murabaha/Sukuk/Wakala facilities, `IFRS 16` lease liabilities, and unfunded Saudi `IAS 19` Employee End-of-Service Benefits (`مكافأة نهاية الخدمة`).
4. **Bridge 04 - `EBITDA` to Free Cash Flow (`FCFF` & `FCFE`) & Dividend Durability Bridge (`DDIS`):**
   Maps `EBITDA -> Operating Cash Flow -> Zakat Paid -> CapEx -> Sukuk/Murabaha Finance Charges -> Net Debt Issuance -> FCFE`, paired with the 10-year `DDIS` principal maturity wall to test dividend sustainability.
5. **Bridge 05 - Zero-Owner-Spend Local AI CLI & Ecosystem Bridge (`Rawased <-> Byan`):**
   Connects the browser terminal to the analyst's own local `claude`, `codex`, `gemini`, or `ollama` CLI via `127.0.0.1:8799` (`/cli/rawased-bridge.py`), and syncs audited per-share Zakat purification ratios to the **Byan (`بيان`)** personal wealth ecosystem.

### 6.2 Required Cloudflare DNS Records for Outbound SPF & DMARC Alignment

Cloudflare Email Routing's default SPF record only lists `include:_spf.mx.cloudflare.net` (which covers inbound forwarding only). To send outbound mail from `binogayel@gmail.com` as `*@rawased.com` without landing in spam, `rawased.com` DNS must include `_spf.google.com` and a `_dmarc` policy:

| Record Type | Host / Name | Required Value | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`TXT`** | `@` (`rawased.com`) | `v=spf1 include:_spf.mx.cloudflare.net include:_spf.google.com ~all` | Authorizes both Cloudflare inbound forwarding and Google outbound SMTP (`smtp.gmail.com`) |
| **`TXT`** | `_dmarc` (`_dmarc.rawased.com`) | `v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:security@rawased.com` | Publishes institutional DMARC alignment policy |
