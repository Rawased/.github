## 4. Bilingual Brand Language & PR Rules (`najdi` + `ghostwrite` + `sharpen`)

### 4.1 Five Iron Linguistic Laws
1. **Single-Language Per Line:** Never mix English and Arabic words inside the same sentence. English copy is crisp, quantitative, and institutional. Arabic copy is authoritative Saudi financial prose aligned with Tadawul (`تداول`), CMA (`هيئة السوق المالية`), ZATCA (`هيئة الزكاة والضريبة والجمارك`), and SOCPA standards.
2. **Primary-Source Authority Over Vendor Summaries:** Lead with audited financial statement footnotes (`إيضاحات القوائم المالية المدققة`), never generic "AI insights" or retail broker summaries.
3. **Zero Advisory Noise (CMA Art. 31 Safe Harbor):** Never output `BUY`, `SELL`, `HOLD`, `OVERWEIGHT`, or price-target recommendations. Rawased computes deterministic mathematical sensitivity ranges and accounting reconciliations; the analyst makes the investment decision.
4. **Zero Punctuation Clutter:** Never use em-dashes or en-dashes in English or Arabic copy. Use periods, colons, or clean structural tables.
5. **Measured Claims Only:** Every product claim names the specific accounting standard (`IFRS 16`, `IAS 19`, `ZATCA 2.5% / 2.577%`), endpoint (`/api/v1/bridges/{symbol}`), or open specification (`rawased-quant-specs`).

## 5. The 5 Institutional Financial & AI Bridges (`/bridges` & `/api/v1/bridges/{symbol}`)

1. **Bridge 01 - Primary Filing vs. Market Feed Reconciliation Bridge (`DISAGREES` Engine):**
   Compares every line item in audited Tadawul PDF footnotes and SEC XBRL disclosures against commercial market feeds (`Delta_bps = |Audited - Vendor| / |Audited| * 10,000`), flagging any divergence above `50 bps` and citing the exact audited note and page number.
2. **Bridge 02 - Saudi ZATCA Corporate Zakat (`2.5%` / `2.577%`) to NOPAT, ROIC & EVA Bridge:**
   Replaces generic global corporate tax assumptions with Saudi mixed-ownership Zakat (`2.5%` Hijri / `2.577%` Gregorian on Saudi/GCC ownership) and foreign income tax (`20%` on non-GCC ownership) to compute true Zakat-Adjusted `NOPAT`, `ROIC`, and Economic Value Added (`EVA`).
3. **Bridge 03 - Enterprise Value (`EV`) to Implied Equity Value Bridge (`CAST` + `IFRS 16` + `IAS 19 EOSB`):**
   Bridges `Market Cap -> EV -> Implied Equity Value -> Fair Value Per Share` by explicitly incorporating Islamic Murabaha/Sukuk/Wakala facilities, `IFRS 16` lease liabilities, and unfunded Saudi `IAS 19` Employee End-of-Service Benefits (`مكافأة نهاية الخدمة`).
4. **Bridge 04 - `EBITDA` to Free Cash Flow (`FCFF` & `FCFE`) & Dividend Durability Bridge (`DDIS`):**
   Maps `EBITDA -> Operating Cash Flow -> Zakat Paid -> CapEx -> Sukuk/Murabaha Finance Charges -> Net Debt Issuance -> FCFE`, paired with the 10-year `DDIS` principal maturity wall to test dividend sustainability.
5. **Bridge 05 - Zero-Owner-Spend Local AI CLI & Ecosystem Bridge (`Rawased <-> Byan`):**
   Connects the browser terminal to the analyst's own local `claude`, `codex`, `gemini`, or `ollama` CLI via `127.0.0.1:8799` (`/cli/rawased-bridge.py`), and syncs audited per-share Zakat purification ratios to the **Byan (`بيان`)** personal wealth ecosystem.
