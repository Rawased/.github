PILLAR 02 · EMPIRICAL VISUAL DIAGNOSTICS (DECKS.MD & BEYNA LAB LAWS)

Four Optical Traps Eliminated Through Visual Inspection.

Per DECKS.md Principle 1 ("Look at it before you ship it") and the Medida visual-tell law ("monotonic lengths off an axis read as a generic bar chart"), we rendered and audited 30+ structural candidates at 1024px, 48px, and 16px. Here are the four optical traps we discovered and how the v3.0 Flagship (`K2`) solves every one:

TRAP 01 · THE "GENERIC BAR CHART" TELL

Both Bars Rising from the Bottom Floor

When both interior bars enter from the bottom wall (`y=85`) and point in the same upward direction, the mark only represents "Up and Up" and reads like a generic two-column bar chart.

SOLVED IN K2 · 180° OPPOSING TOP-DOWN & BOTTOM-UP INTERLOCK

In K2, the Left Vault Arm plunges DOWN from the top ceiling (`y=11 → 55.5`) while the Right Vault Arm surges UP from the bottom floor (`y=89 → 44.5`), creating true opposing market vectors (`↓ ↑`).

TRAP 02 · THE "AUDIO PLUG / SYRINGE" TELL

Stepped Wider Boxes or Thin 3px Needle Tips on 8px Pipes

When an `8px` pipe turns a corner from the outer frame and narrows into a `3px` thin wick tip (or bulges to `12px` in the middle), human visual perception reads a syringe needle or RCA audio jack.

SOLVED IN K2 · 100% UNIFORM 8PX ARCHITECTURAL STROKE WEIGHT

Every structural wall, interior beam, and kinetic impulse facet in K2 maintains an exact `8.0px` width (`x=37.5..45.5` and `x=54.5..62.5`). Zero thin needles, zero bulges.

TRAP 03 · THE "! i PUNCTUATION" TELL

Square Dots Separated Above/Below Vertical Stems

Placing a flat `8x8` square below the left descending bar and above the right ascending bar causes the brain's typographic parser to read an exclamation mark and lowercase `i` (`! i`).

SOLVED IN K2 · 45° KINETIC CHEVRON IMPULSE FACETS

By shearing the beam terminals and impulse facets at a crisp 45° angle (`polygon 37.5,52.5 → 45.5,60.5`), the eye reads directional velocity and stock breakout/pullback vectors instead of punctuation.

TRAP 04 · THE "G t LETTERFORM" TELL

90° Horizontal Crossbars on Vertical Columns

Adding perpendicular horizontal ticks across the vertical columns causes the outer loop + crossbar to read as the Latin letters `G t` at `24px` scale.

SOLVED IN K2 · UNBROKEN 9PX CENTRAL OBSERVATORY MERIDIAN SLIT

The central channel (`x=45.5..54.5`) runs unobstructed from `y=11` to `y=89`, acting as the precision optical slit of a financial observatory (`مرصد مالي`).
