## Rawased (`رواصد`) — Saudi (`Tadawul`) & US (`NYSE / Nasdaq / SEC`) Financial Filing & Quant Terminal

**Rawased (`rawased.sa` · `rawased.com`)** is an institutional-grade Saudi and US financial filing, valuation, and quantitative analytics platform operating strictly under the **CFA Institute Material Public Information Mosaic Theory** (*"describes, never advises"*).

| Pillar | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Market Coverage** | **Saudi Exchange (`Tadawul / Nomu`)** + **US Equities (`NYSE / Nasdaq / SEC EDGAR XBRL`)** + **Sovereign Yield Curves & Macro Rates (`SAMA` · `FRED`)** |
| **Core Terminal Functions** | Company Valuation Multiples · **`CAST`** (Capital Structure & Sukuk/Debt Stack) · **`DDIS`** (Debt Maturity Distribution) · Corporate **Zakat vs. Tax Bridge** · Factor & Quant Screener · Headline NLP Polarity |
| **Regulatory Safe Harbor** | Factual quantitative synthesis under **CMA Securities Business Regulations (Article 31)** Non-Advisory Safe Harbor & **Saudi PDPL (`Royal Decree M/19`)** |
| **Licensed & Statutory Feeds** | Saudi market data provided by **SAHMK (`بيانات السوق مقدمة من سهمك`)** · US market data via **Alpaca IEX (`feed=iex`)** · Statutory filings via **SEC EDGAR** & **Saudi Exchange** |
| **Founders** | **Waleed BinOgayel** (`waleed@rawased.com`) & **Abdulmohsen Alhassan** (`abdulmohsen@rawased.com`) · Support: `help@rawased.com` |
